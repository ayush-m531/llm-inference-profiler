========================================================================================
B. GLOBAL MODEL, and what the ACTIVATION features add
========================================================================================
A global model with layer and scheme as features will show a high R2 -
but those two explain most of the variance on their own, and the lookup
table already knows both. The number that matters is the GAIN from adding
the activation features. A high global R2 without a gain is the
pooled-correlation trap in a new form.

     model    config only   + activation       gain
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
     ridge          0.254          0.395     +0.141
    forest          0.962          0.960     -0.002

READING THIS: ridge cannot represent layer and scheme as CATEGORICAL
variables - it treats layer 0-11 as a linear number - so its config-only
fit is poor and the activation features partly act as proxies for layer
identity. That inflates ridge's apparent gain. The forest splits on layer
and scheme properly, so ITS gain is the honest measure of what the
activation contributes beyond what the lookup table already encodes.
